Concrete Moisture Testing and Diamond Grinding Prep

Bare garage concrete often looks smooth and troweled tight—almost glazed—which leaves almost no mechanical grip for a resin coat. Preparation turns that dense surface into a clean, lightly textured profile similar to medium sandpaper.

Three non-negotiable prep steps

  1. Check for vapor pressure and existing sealers: Tape a clear plastic square tightly to several clean areas of the floor for 24–48 hours; condensation underneath or darkened concrete signals upward moisture that needs quantitative testing before coating. Drop a spoonful of water on bare concrete too—if water beads instead of soaking in, an old sealer or oil film is blocking adhesion.
  2. Diamond grind rather than relying solely on acid etch: Walk-behind diamond grinders with HEPA dust shrouds strip surface laitance, paint splatters, and curing membranes uniformly without saturating the slab with water.
  3. Vacuum thoroughly and repair spalls: Residual fine concrete dust acts as a bond breaker; patch pitted salt damage and routed cracks before the base coat goes down.
