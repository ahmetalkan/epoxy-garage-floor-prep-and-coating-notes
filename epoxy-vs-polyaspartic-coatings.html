Epoxy vs Polyaspartic Garage Floor Coatings

Many commercial installers combine both chemistries rather than picking only one: a slow-wetting epoxy base coat that penetrates deep into concrete pores followed by a UV-stable polyaspartic clear seal coat.

Property100%-solids epoxyPolyaspartic (aliphatic polyurea)
Working time (pot life)Longer working window allows deep pore penetrationVery short pot life; demands experienced fast rolling
UV color stabilityTends to amber/yellow where direct sunlight hits the apronResists UV yellowing near open garage doors
Cure speedUsually requires multi-day wait before parking vehiclesCures rapidly across a wider temperature range
Moisture toleranceMoisture-vapor-barrier epoxy primers handle damp slabs wellDirect-to-concrete single-day coats can blister on high-moisture slabs
